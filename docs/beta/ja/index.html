Beta: what to try

Thank you for testing Giftokeep. The app's words are given in Japanese, exactly as you see them on screen.

Giftokeep keeps track of gifts: what you've received (頂いた), what you've presented (お贈りした), and what you're planning (予定). It opens with a few examples (the card on ホーム says これはサンプルです). Remove them any time with 削除 on that card.

1. Log a gift you received and give one back (お返し)

贈り物 tab → 贈り物を追加 → choose 頂いた, pick who it's from, enter the gift, 保存. Open the gift and tap お返しする to plan a gift in return. Move it along 予定 → 購入済み → お贈りした.

2. Plan an occasion with a budget

イベント tab → open お歳暮 (or クリスマス), set 予算（任意）, and add gift ideas for a few people. Watch the budget bar update as gifts go from 予定 to 購入済み.

3. Check iCloud (from version 1.2)

With iCloud on, add something, then delete the app and reinstall it from TestFlight. Your gifts should come back, with no duplicates. If you have a second iPhone or iPad on the same Apple ID, changes should show up there too. You can see the version at the bottom of ヘルプ.

The paid part (アンロック)

The free version holds 4 people (相手), the examples included. Adding more shows Giftokeepをアンロック: a one-time purchase in yen, no subscription.

In TestFlight you won't be charged. Apple runs purchases in a test mode, so tap アンロック and confirm. A test unlock can't be undone, so the paywall won't appear again for you afterwards.

  2. What's your first impression? What did you expect Giftokeep to do, and did it?
  3. Did anything feel illogical or strange? For example, a button that did something unexpected, a screen where you didn't know what to do next, or a word that didn't fit.
  4. Was it easy to use? Which of the three things above was easiest, and where did you hesitate or get stuck?

Short notes are perfect. A screenshot with one line (“this word feels odd”) helps us most.
