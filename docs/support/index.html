Common questions

Who are the people already in the app?
Examples, so the app isn't empty the first time you open it. Tap Remove on the "These are examples" card on the Home screen and they disappear, along with their gifts. People you added yourself are never touched.
What does "≈" in front of a price mean?
It's an estimate Giftokeep filled in for you, from what you paid before or from typical prices where the gift was bought. Tap the price to confirm it or type the real one. Totals that include an estimate say so.
Why aren't amounts in different currencies added up?
Because there's no honest exchange rate for a gift from years ago. Each currency is totalled on its own.
Which holidays appear under Events?
Christmas, Valentine's Day, Easter, Mother's Day and Father's Day, on the dates used in your region. For example, gifts on Christmas Eve in Germany and Austria, Mothering Sunday in the UK, and Father's Day on Ascension Day in Germany. Add your own events, like a wedding or a Secret Santa, with the + button.
Does Giftokeep sync between my devices?
Not yet. Your data stays on the iPhone where you entered it and is included in your iPhone's backups.
Can the person I'm buying for see what I planned?
Only if they look at your phone. Giftokeep sends nothing anywhere, and it will never put a gift's name in a notification.
