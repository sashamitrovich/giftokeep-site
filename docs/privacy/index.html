Privacy policy

Giftokeep keeps your gifts, people and plans on your iPhone. It does not collect, store or send any personal data to its developer.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What Giftokeep stores, and where

The people you add, the gifts you log (given and received), their prices and occasions, your ideas and your event plans and budgets are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded. There is no Giftokeep server.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, this data is included in that backup the same way as every other app's data, under Apple's terms.

No account, no analytics, no tracking

There is no sign-in. Giftokeep contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Price estimates

When Giftokeep suggests a price, it works it out on your device: from prices you entered before, or from a built-in table of typical prices for the region where a gift was bought. The gift's name is never sent anywhere to get an estimate.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. To show its price and complete a purchase, Giftokeep asks the App Store, and Apple tells it only whether the unlock is owned. Giftokeep never sees your payment details or Apple Account, and sends none of your gifts, people or plans.

Your region

The region you choose for purchases, used for currency and price estimates, is saved on your device in iOS's standard settings storage.

Permissions

This version of Giftokeep asks for no permissions: no Contacts, Photos, location or notifications. If a future version needs one, it will ask you first, and this policy will say what it is used for before that version is released.

Notifications

When reminders arrive in a future version, they will never name the gift itself, because the person the gift is for may see your lock screen.

Children

Giftokeep collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete people, gifts and your own events in the app (touch and hold a gift; swipe an event or a person), or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
